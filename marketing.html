Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up Kavirox

How long does it take to set up our coaching centre?

Less than 15 minutes. You download our Excel template, paste your student names and parent phone numbers, and upload it. The system automatically creates roll numbers, batches, and parent logins.

Do parents need to remember a password?

No! Parents log in exclusively using their registered mobile number and a 6-digit OTP received via SMS or WhatsApp. No passwords to forget or reset.

Can other coaching centres see our student or financial data?

Never. Every centre has a strict tenant partition in the database. Our automated tenant isolation test mathematically guarantees zero cross-centre data leakage.

What happens if we decide to stop using Kavirox?

You always own your data. You can download all student profiles, fee ledgers, and attendance records as an Excel/CSV file with a single click anytime.
