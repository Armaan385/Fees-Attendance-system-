FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Admission Guidelines & Policies

Are hostel and accommodation facilities available for outstation students?

Yes. We have verified boys and girls residential hostels within 200 meters of both our Kota and Greater Noida campuses with 24x7 biometric security, healthy food, and AC rooms.

How is daily student attendance communicated to parents?

Within 30 seconds of student check-in via their digital barcode ID badge, an automated attendance SMS and WhatsApp notification is sent directly to the registered parent mobile number.

What is the refund and fee cancellation policy?

We maintain 100% compliance with consumer protection guidelines. Pro-rata refund of tuition fees is provided if a student withdraws within 30 days of batch commencement, minus study kit costs.
